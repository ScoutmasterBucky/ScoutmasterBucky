Airplane Structure is discontinued. It has been replaced by Aviation. (See Discontinued awards.)
Those who earned it before it was discontinued may still wear it, but it can no longer be earned.


Airplane Structure merit badge
Status: Discontinued BSA Advancement ID: n/a
Created: 1942 Replaced: Aviation (original)
Replaced in: 1952 Replaced by: Aviation (current)

Airplane Structure was first offered in 1942, along with Aeronautics, Aerodynamics, and Airplane Design, to replace the original Aviation merit badge. They were all replaced by the current Aviation merit badge in 1952.
