2023 merit badge rankings: A look into Scouts’ current interests and achievements

By Gina Circelli | February 13, 2024 | 54 Comments

Advertisement

If you’re a Scout parent or leader who likes to keep a finger on the pulse of youth interests, the list you’ve been waiting for is here!

Once a year, we share our roundup of the most-earned merit badges from the last calendar year. And we just got the results from 2023.

In 2022, we saw the Citizenship in Society merit badge top the list as it launched its first full year as the newest Eagle-required badge. This year, things look similar. That is at least until we get farther down the list. Let’s take a look at the 2023 merit badge rankings.

The top 10 most-earned merit badges of 2023

Bolded below are badges that flipped rankings slightly but still remain close to where they were last year. To compare, check out our 2022 merit badge rankings.

2023 Rank Merit Badge
1 Citizenship in Society
2 First Aid
3 Swimming
4 Environmental Science
5 Citizenship in the World
6 Camping
7 Citizenship in the Nation
8 Personal Fitness 
9 Communication 
10 Cooking 

Yup, this list looks very close to last year’s. But now let’s view the list through another lens.

An alternative top 10

If we remove the merit badges Scouts are required to earn to achieve Eagle Scout, we see another top 10 list. There are more than 100 other badges Scouts can choose among. Here’s what topped those rankings.

2023 Rank Merit Badge
1 Fingerprinting
2 Chess
3 Archery
4 Rifle Shooting
5 Leatherwork
6 Wood Carving
7 Kayaking
8 Art
9 Fishing
10 Wilderness Survival

From last year, the Chess merit badge jumped up five spots! Otherwise, the changes were minor.

The rarest badges of 2023

There’s a realm of Scouts who live to earn the rarest badges. And we’ve got a list for them, too!

2023 Rank Merit Badge
129 Dentistry
130 Gardening
131 Energy
132 Stamp Collecting

135 Surveying

137 Drafting

The Veterinary Medicine and Truck Transportation merit badges jumped out of the bottom 10, up 14 and 13 spots respectively. These least-earned badges saw a bit of shake up this year, proving Scouts’ interests change even from one year to the next.

Top gains of the year

Some badges made a leap in the ranks. In fact, the Collections merit badge was earned by 1,000 more Scouts in 2023!

Here are more notable gains.
