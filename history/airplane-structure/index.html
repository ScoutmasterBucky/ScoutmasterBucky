The Story

The "Air Scouts" program, established in 1941, offered the Ace Award as its highest program level recognition. Despite lower membership numbers, the Ace Award maintained a similar consistency to its program equivalent; the standard Boy Scouts program and the Eagle Scout award, at around 3 to 4 percent of all potential members receiving the award.

Trivia

Timeline

1942 The Airplane Structure merit badge is introduced. The first Airplane Structure merit badges are earned: 220 that year. The earliest known Airplane Structure pamphlet printing (Type 4BL).

1944 All-time high: 1,738 Airplane Structure merit badges earned. Best ranking: 66th of 111 merit badges.

1952 Last year the Airplane Structure merit badge is offered; it is replaced by Aviation.

What do the patch types mean?
Type C
Tan narrow border crimped (1936 - 1942). The same crimped edge with a narrow border of about 1/8 inch, on tan cloth.
Type D
Fine twill (1942 - 1946). Wartime badges on lighter, finer-weave cloth (fine twill and war cloth) while heavier cloth went to the war effort.
Type E
Khaki narrow border crimped (1947 - 1960). Back to heavier cloth after the war, now in khaki.

Dates are approximate and can vary by badge.
