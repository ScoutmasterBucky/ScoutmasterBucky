What do the patch types mean?
Type H
Plastic back (1972 - 2002). A plastic stiffener on the back. Collectors tell them apart by the back, which can be clear, white, or a very light blue plastic, with waffle-textured and smooth molded versions. These are sorted into variations H1 through H5.
Type I
Computer designed (1993 - 1995). Flat-edged badges with machine (computer) embroidery and a wider border. Only some badges were made this way.
Type J
Scout Stuff back (2002 - 2009). The "Scout Stuff" Supply Division logo printed on the back.
Type K
BSA 100th anniversary back (2010 - 2014). The BSA 2010 anniversary logo printed on the back.
Type L
Since 1910 back (2015 - 2025). The "Since 1910" logo printed on the back, in large and small logo versions.
Type M
Scouting America back (2025 - current). The Scouting America logo printed on the back. Wildland Fire Management and Competitive Gaming, both new in 2026, start with Type M.

Dates are approximate and can vary by badge.
