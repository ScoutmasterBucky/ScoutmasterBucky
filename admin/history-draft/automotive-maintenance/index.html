The Story

The Auto Mechanics merit badge was announced in the October 1991 edition of Scouting Magazine and in 2008 quietly changed to the current title of Automotive Maintenance. A new merit badge design accompanied the August 1, 2008 release of the new color Merit Badge pamphlets that saw Auto Mechnics morph immediately into Automotive Maintenance. Total merit badges earned numbers, maintained by the National BSA, reflect these two merit badges as one.merit badge in their records.

Trivia

Timeline

1991 The Automotive Maintenance merit badge is introduced.

1993 First known count: 3,566 Automotive Maintenance merit badges earned.

2001 Best ranking: 55th of 129 merit badges.

2004 All-time high: 8,892 Automotive Maintenance merit badges earned.

2008 The earliest known Automotive Maintenance pamphlet printing (Type 10A). Last year the Automotive Maintenance merit badge is offered; it is replaced by Automotive Maintenance current.

What do the patch types mean?
Type H
Plastic back (1972 - 2002). A plastic stiffener on the back. Collectors tell them apart by the back, which can be clear, white, or a very light blue plastic, with waffle-textured and smooth molded versions. These are sorted into variations H1 through H5.
Type I
Computer designed (1993 - 1995). Flat-edged badges with machine (computer) embroidery and a wider border. Only some badges were made this way.
Type J
Scout Stuff back (2002 - 2009). The "Scout Stuff" Supply Division logo printed on the back.
Type K
BSA 100th anniversary back (2010 - 2014). The BSA 2010 anniversary logo printed on the back.
Type L
Since 1910 back (2015 - 2025). The "Since 1910" logo printed on the back, in large and small logo versions.

Dates are approximate and can vary by badge.
