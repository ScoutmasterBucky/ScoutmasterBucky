The Story

The Agribusiness merit badge was short-lived and is one of the shortest run merit badge titles as well as total earned during its tenure in the merit badge offerings. Agribusiness was introduced along with 5 other merit badges in 1987; the first new offerings since 1982 (Backpacking).

Trivia

Timeline

1987 The Agribusiness merit badge is introduced. The earliest known Agribusiness pamphlet printing (Type 9A-1).

1993 First known count: 1,138 Agribusiness merit badges earned. All-time high: 1,138 Agribusiness merit badges earned. Best ranking: 117th of 123 merit badges.

1995 Last year the Agribusiness merit badge is offered.

What do the patch types mean?
Type H
Plastic back (1972 - 2002). A plastic stiffener on the back. Collectors tell them apart by the back, which can be clear, white, or a very light blue plastic, with waffle-textured and smooth molded versions. These are sorted into variations H1 through H5.
Type I
Computer designed (1993 - 1995). Flat-edged badges with machine (computer) embroidery and a wider border. Only some badges were made this way.
