Trivia

Timeline

1972 The Mammals merit badge is introduced. The first Mammals merit badges are earned: 12,600 that year. The earliest known Mammals pamphlet printing (Type 7).

1975 All-time high: 29,864 Mammals merit badges earned.

1981 Best ranking: 23rd of 134 merit badges.

1987 Last year the Mammals merit badge is offered; it is replaced by Mammal Study.

What do the patch types mean?
Type H
Plastic back (1972 - 2002). A plastic stiffener on the back. Collectors tell them apart by the back, which can be clear, white, or a very light blue plastic, with waffle-textured and smooth molded versions. These are sorted into variations H1 through H5.
