1911 requirements

  1. Know and name fifty minerals.
  2. Know, name and describe the fourteen great divisions of the earth's crust (according to Geikie).
  3. Define watershed, delta, drift, fault, glacier, terrace, stratum, dip; and identify ten different kinds of rock.
  4. Describe methods for mine ventilation and safety devices.

Source: The Official Handbook for Boys (1911)

Timeline

1911 The Mining merit badge is introduced. It is one of the original 57 merit badges.

1912 The first Mining merit badges are earned: 6 that year.

1919 The earliest known Mining pamphlet printing (Type 1B-1). Best ranking: 31st of 59 merit badges.

1926 All-time high: 752 Mining merit badges earned.

1937 Last year the Mining merit badge is offered; it is replaced by Rocks & Minerals.

What do the patch types mean?
Type A
Square (1911 - 1933). Embroidered on tan cloth and cut into squares. The Scout folded the edges under when sewing it on.
Type B
Wide border crimped (1934 - 1935). The maker folded the edge under and pressed it round, leaving a wide cloth border. Made on depression cloth and heavyweight cloth.
Type C
Tan narrow border crimped (1936 - 1942). The same crimped edge with a narrow border of about 1/8 inch, on tan cloth.

Dates are approximate and can vary by badge.
