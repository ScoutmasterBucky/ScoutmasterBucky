Trivia

Timeline

1937 The Rocks and Minerals merit badge is introduced. The first Rocks and Minerals merit badges are earned: 19 that year. The earliest known Rocks and Minerals pamphlet printing (Type 3D).

1952 All-time high: 2,354 Rocks and Minerals merit badges earned.

1953 Best ranking: 61st of 105 merit badges. Last year the Rocks and Minerals merit badge is offered; it is replaced by Geology.

What do the patch types mean?
Type C
Tan narrow border crimped (1936 - 1942). The same crimped edge with a narrow border of about 1/8 inch, on tan cloth.
Type D
Fine twill (1942 - 1946). Wartime badges on lighter, finer-weave cloth (fine twill and war cloth) while heavier cloth went to the war effort.
Type E
Khaki narrow border crimped (1947 - 1960). Back to heavier cloth after the war, now in khaki.

Dates are approximate and can vary by badge.
