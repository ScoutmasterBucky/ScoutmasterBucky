1911 requirements

  1. Invent and patent some useful article;
  2. Show a working drawing or model of the same.

Source: The Official Handbook for Boys (1911)

Timeline

1911 The Invention merit badge is introduced. It is one of the original 57 merit badges.

1913 The first Invention merit badge is earned.

1914 All-time high: 8 Invention merit badges earned. Best ranking: 52nd of 58 merit badges.

1917 Last year the Invention merit badge is offered.
